# Tokopedia — Customer Retention & Promo Efficiency

**Data Analytics Capstone Project 3**  
**Author:** Adilah Adzhani Amanda  
**Institution:** Purwadhika Digital Technology School  
**Analysis Period:** 2023–2024

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Tokopedia customer retention and promotional efficiency to identify opportunities for improving repeat purchases and optimizing first-order promotional spending.

## Business Objectives
- Measure customer repeat purchase behavior within 90 days.
- Analyze promotional spending across customer segments.
- Compare customer value and acquisition channel performance.
- Examine relationships between delivery performance, customer service, and retention.
- Evaluate promotional efficiency through CLV proxy and financial scenarios.

## Datasets
The analysis uses five datasets: Users, Sellers, Transactions, Deliveries, and Customer Service Tickets.

## Methodology
Data cleaning and validation, feature engineering, 90-day repeat order analysis, promotional segmentation, cohort analysis, customer value comparison, acquisition channel analysis, customer experience analysis, CLV proxy estimation, and promotional budget simulations.

## Key Findings
- **47,350** customers were eligible for the complete 90-day observation period.
- **77.22%** of eligible customers made a repeat purchase within 90 days.
- **81.13%** of analyzed first-order promotional spending was concentrated in the High Promo segment.
- The Medium Promo segment had the highest average 90-day Customer Value, at approximately **IDR 8.26 million** per customer.
- On-time delivery and the absence of early customer service tickets were associated with higher subsequent repeat purchase rates.
- A modeled **10% reduction** in High Promo incentives represents approximately **IDR 211.28 million** in potential promotional expenditure reduction, assuming unchanged customer behavior.

## Recommendations
1. Test a controlled 5% reduction in High Promo incentives.
2. Monitor acquisition channels using retention, promotional spending, and customer value metrics.
3. Investigate delivery delays and improve delivery performance.
4. Review customer service ticket categories and resolution effectiveness.

## Limitations
The findings are observational and do not establish causality. CLV proxy estimates use assumed contribution margins rather than actual lifetime profitability. Modeled promotional savings are not guaranteed realized savings.
